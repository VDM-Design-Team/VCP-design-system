### `ConnectionCard` — link an account to an outside service (November 2026)

New, built to Figma's `_Personal_Info_Item_Jira_Connect`: a brand logo tile, the service's name, a one-line status and one action button. Generic on purpose — the status, the button's words and what the press does are the caller's, so "Not connected / Connect", "Connected as … / Disconnect" and "Connecting…" are all the same card. It only reports the press.
