### `MonthField` — a month and year from a grid (November 2026)

New, built to Figma's `_SuperAdmin_Metric_Month_Selector`: a read-only field that reads "September 2026" with a trailing chevron, and the `DatePicker` in `month` mode in a `Popover` beneath it. `onChange` reports the first of the chosen month (ISO). `DateField`'s sibling for a month with no day.
