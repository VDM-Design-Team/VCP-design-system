### `PlanningPage` — a place for a notice above the sections (November 2026)

New optional `banner` (`Banner`'s props): a notice between the filters and the Cycle and Backlog sections, the column's width, for something about the plan as a whole that the person should act on, such as people booked past their capacity cap. Figma draws none; what it says and when it shows is the page's. Without it the page is as before. A new story, `WithABanner`, checks where it sits.
