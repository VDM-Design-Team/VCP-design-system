### `PageTitle`: the title and its actions share a centre line (November 2026)

The band aligned its children to the top, so a one-line title (a 26 high line of text) sat about 5 above the centre of 36 high actions, such as the super admin dashboard's filters. They now share a centre line. With a `subtitle` the title block is two lines tall and the actions stay at the top, as before.
