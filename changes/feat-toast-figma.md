### `Toast` — the Figma design: a neutral card, tone in the icon and timer bar (October 2026)

The tinted card is gone. A Toast is a white `surface.elevated` card with a `stroke.subtle` border, 16 padding and the `sm` radius, and it hugs its content up to `max-w-sm`. The tone shows only in the filled glyph and the solid timer bar (the accent's `outline.border.default`): `info-fill`, `check-circle-fill`, `warning-circle-fill` for warning and `warning-fill` for danger. Title is 14 semibold `text.primary`, the message is 14 medium `text.secondary`. The action is the standard small secondary Button, centred on the card, and the dismiss control is a neutral round icon button. Adds the `info-fill` and `warning-circle-fill` glyphs.
