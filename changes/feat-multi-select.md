### `MultiSelect` — several choices from a fixed list; `Chip` gets a neutral tone (October 2026)

New component, to Figma's filter fields and menus: a dropdown field that shows the picks as removable chips or a summary, over a panel of checkbox rows with optional group headings. `confirm` adds the status menu's Clear all / Apply footer, drafting the ticks until Apply. It is a select-only combobox with full keyboard support.

`Chip` gains `tone="neutral"` (default `brand`), the grey pill a value wears inside a field.
