### `Modal` — 18px title, round close button, optional subtitle and footer (October 2026)

The title drops from 20 to 18 (`body-lg-semibold`) and the close button is a circle. New `showDescription` and `showFooter` props (both default `true`) hide the subtitle and the footer actions. The gap between header and body is 12 under a subtitle and 4 without one.
