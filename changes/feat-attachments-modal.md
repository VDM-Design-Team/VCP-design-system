### `AttachmentsModal` (November 2026)

New **`AttachmentsModal`** (Figma's `Attachment_Modal`): the files already on an Added Value as `FileAttachment` cards (hover ✕ removes, clicking a card opens its preview in `AttachmentViewer` over the dialog), a `Dropzone`, and **Upload**, which sends only the files just picked and waits until there are some.
